GlovesOn
What a judge can check

Posts the goods receipt straight into SAP — here a mock S/4HANA that speaks SAP's released OData contract, one setting away from a live tenant.

What it does

  • X-CSRF-Token: Fetch handshake, A_MaterialDocumentHeader, movement types 101 / 102
  • Read-back names quantity, unit, material description and bin; nothing posts without a spoken yes
  • Wrong receipt is reversed, never deleted — both documents stay
  • The same receipt twice is refused in the gateway, because real S/4HANA would accept it

Not verified

  • Email, calls and notes are demo records; nothing reaches a real person
