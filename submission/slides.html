05 · THE DISCIPLINE
A write you can trust

Four guardrails, each one deliberate.

1
The read-back names four things.

Quantity, unit, material description, bin. The description is load-bearing.

2
Nothing posts without a spoken yes.

execution_mode: hold — the agent waits for the ERP instead of narrating success.

3
A duplicate is refused.

The same receipt within two minutes is caught — by a guard in the gateway, because real S/4HANA would accept it twice.

4
Nothing is deleted — it is reversed.

A wrong receipt gets a reversal document (102 against a 101). Both stay; the audit trail ties each to its spoken confirmation.

Reasoning in full: docs/adr/0002-confirm-before-write.md

10 · HONEST LIMITS
Named, not hidden

What this does not do yet.

The posting is made by a service user, not the worker — SAP cannot yet say who did it. The missing Clean Core piece: principal propagation.
Noise robustness is untested — voice_focus is not yet enabled, on a floor that is loud by definition.
The demo serves one session at a time, by design.
Turkish is understood on input, but the agent cannot answer in Turkish yet (TTS support pending).
Naming the gap is the credibility. All four are written up in the repo.
