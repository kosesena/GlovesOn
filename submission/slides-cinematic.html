06 · THE DISCIPLINE
Asking is easy. Finishing is the hard part.

Five guardrails, each one deliberate.

1
The read-back names four things.

Quantity, unit, material description, bin. The description is load-bearing.

2
Writes require a confirmed draft.

The gateway validates the exact draft. Spoken consent is reported by the agent; results appear only after the tool returns.

3
A duplicate is refused.

The same receipt within two minutes is caught — by a guard in the gateway, because real S/4HANA would accept it twice.

4
Nothing is deleted — it is reversed.

A wrong receipt gets a reversal document, 102 against a 101. Both stay, because that is what a ledger is.

5
Every document answers why it exists.

Click a posted row: the sentence the worker confirmed, and the second it happened.

Reasoning in full: docs/adr/0002-confirm-before-write.md

11 · HONEST LIMITS
Named, not hidden

What this does not do yet.

The ERP is a mock — the OData contract and the CSRF handshake are real; a live tenant was tried and the Business Accelerator Hub sandbox answered 401 on all four required services (10 September). Nothing here has run against real S/4HANA.
No employee sign-in or SAP principal propagation — session capabilities protect tool calls, but do not establish a worker's identity.
Noise robustness is unmeasured — voice_focus: far-field is set, but a config change is a claim, not a measurement.
Session events are isolated; warehouse records are shared — this is a demonstration, not a tenant-isolated production system.
The supported product language is English; other languages are outside the demo scope.
Naming the gap is the credibility. Every one is written up in the repo, and the judge guide ends with them.
