What this does not do — said here, not left for you to find

  • The ERP is a mock. The OData contract and the CSRF handshake are real; no claim is made that this has run against a live tenant.
  • Nobody is authenticated, and the posting is made by a service user — SAP cannot say who spoke. Closing that needs principal propagation; it is the largest gap in docs/clean-core.md.
  • Noise robustness is unmeasured. voice_focus: far-field is set, but a config change is a claim, not a measurement.
  • One session at a time, by design. Two people speaking at once would share a session reference.
