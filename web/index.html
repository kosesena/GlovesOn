How GlovesOn works

Every delivery has to end up in SAP. That used to mean gloves off and a walk to a terminal — here it takes one spoken sentence.

GlovesOn · Built for the receiving floor.Goods receipt · Stock · ReversalSAP OData · Demo environment Voice Agent Hackathon / 2026

01 / RECEIVE

Check in a delivery.

Say what arrived and how many. The bin, the unit and the description are looked up for you.

YOU“Forty M8 bolts arrived.”

YOUR GUIDE“Forty pieces of hex bolt M8x40 into bin A-03-02 — confirm?”

YOU“Yes.”

Receipt posted · stock 240 → 280

01 Check in a delivery
02 / FIND

Find material and stock.

Ask where a material lives and how much is on hand. A lookup — nothing changes.

YOU“Where are the M8 bolts?”

YOUR GUIDE“Bin A-03-02 — 240 on hand.”

No confirmation needed · stock untouched

02 Find material and stock
03 / REVIEW

Reverse the right receipt.

A wrong receipt is corrected with a reversal document. Nothing is ever deleted.

YOU“That last receipt was wrong.”

YOUR GUIDE“Reversing it — forty hex bolts back out of A-03-02. Confirm?”

YOU“Yes.”

Reversal posted · both documents stay

03 Reverse the right receipt
