GlovesOn — design system

Three surfaces, one identity. Everything the deck, the cover image and the demo video need, in one file that opens in a browser and needs nothing installed.

SOURCE OF TRUTH is the app: web/index.html for the site and the console, submission/slides.html for the deck. This file mirrors those tokens so a video editor or a slide never has to eyedrop a running app. If a colour changes there, change it here.

The three surfaces

The cream ground is the world — landing, lobby, the receiving floor. The dark console is the machine. The white document is the thing that matters, and it is the only surface that is nearly pure white: on a dark terminal, contrast says this is the record without a caption. Never soften that contrast to make a slide prettier.

Rules that are not taste

  • Mono is for ERP data, never for prose. Numbers, bins, document numbers, movement types. Instructional sentences set in mono read as console output and stop being read.
  • Orange marks a hard rule or a limit, not decoration. If it is orange, something is being refused, awaited, or admitted.
  • Green is the completed write. Do not use it for a pending one — the whole point of the amber state is that nothing has happened yet.
  • Never soften the document's white to blend it into a slide. The contrast between the dark console and the white paper is the argument.
  • Never show a POSTED stamp beside an empty field. If the deck or the video needs a document still, use one with every field filled.

Motion

Two named animations carry the whole demo, and both have a reason. thud (.45s, cubic-bezier(.2,1.4,.4,1)) drops the stamp with an overshoot — the sound a stamp would make. pulse (1.1s, ease-in-out, infinite) marks anything that is waiting. In the posting sequence the stamp lands first, the document number appears 280 ms later and the stock counts over 800 ms: all at once reads as a screen refresh, in sequence it reads as a posting completing. Do not compress that in the edit — the two seconds are the product.
